Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch templates later?

Yes. Open your portfolio in the builder and pick another template under Template & Settings. Your content stays the same.

Are templates fully customizable?

All of your content is editable in the builder. You can also choose an accent colour and show or hide sections.

Are portfolios mobile-friendly?

Every template is responsive and adapts to phones, tablets and desktops. The builder preview lets you check each size.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. Every template is free to use.
